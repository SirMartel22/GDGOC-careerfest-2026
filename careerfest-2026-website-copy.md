### 8. PROJECT GUIDELINES

**Section label:** `// rules`

**Headline:**
> How to Build Right.

**Guidelines:**

**01 — Use Google Tools**
Your project must incorporate at least one Google AI or Cloud product. The workshop will show you which ones to use.

**02 — Solve a Real Problem**
Pick an SDG challenge that matters to you. Build something that actually addresses it — not just an idea, a solution.

**03 — Submit What Works**
Your project should be functional. A live link, demo, or prototype that someone can actually interact with.

**04 — Original Work Only**
Everything submitted must be built during the challenge window. Pre-built projects won't be considered.

**05 — Anyone Can Build**
You don't need to be a CS student. Every field has something to contribute. Bring your perspective.
